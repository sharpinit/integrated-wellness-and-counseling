If someone sent you here

If a trusted provider, friend or relative referred you, you’re in the right place. We’ll keep the next step simple and private.

What we work on

  • Staying composed before a big moment
  • Winding down after long, demanding days
  • Big transitions: an injury, a career change or what comes next
  • A calming routine while you recover from an illness or injury, alongside your medical care

Discretion comes first

  • Your name, your sessions and your reasons for coming stay private.
  • We never name clients, and we never use your story in our marketing.
  • We work around demanding schedules, including travel.
  • Therapy is available by telehealth when you’re in Nevada, Utah or Idaho.

How it works: 2 Pathways

  • Pathway 1: Start with Dr. Sharp

    1. Private consultation. You meet Dr. Sharp first. He learns what you want to work on.

    2. Your plan. NeuralChek and BrainTap sessions, focused counseling, or both.

    3. Your sessions. A certified associate guides BrainTap sessions in our Henderson office, and counseling is in person or by telehealth.

  • Pathway 2: Start with NeuralChek

    1. NeuralChek reading. A certified associate guides a five-minute heart rate variability reading.

    2. Review your results. We walk you through what your reading shows about how your body handles stress.

    3. Your first BrainTap session. If it’s a good fit, your associate helps you choose a session and guides you through 20 to 30 minutes of deep relaxation.
