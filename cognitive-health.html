Cognitive health and vitality

Staying sharp is about more than memory.

It’s about confidence, independence and peace of mind, for you and the people who care about you.

Your brain’s natural rhythms play a part in clarity, mood and resilience. Caring for them is part of caring for yourself.

How we can help

  • Easing stress and worry about getting older
  • Building calming routines, including before bed
  • Adjusting to retirement, loss, a move or new health news
  • Supporting you as you care for a spouse or parent
  • Counseling with Dr. Sharp for grief, depression, anxiety, addiction and past trauma

A word about memory

If you or someone you love notices changes in memory or thinking, start with your physician. Some causes are treatable, and a medical evaluation is the right first step.

We’re happy to work alongside your treating physicians and healthcare providers to help you be your best.
