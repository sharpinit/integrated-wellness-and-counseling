How therapy with Dr. Sharp works

What you think, what you feel and what you do are all connected. Change one and the others start to shift.

Therapy is where you see those connections clearly. Then you build practical ways to change them.

  • Focused on your goals. Dr. Sharp favors brief, practical approaches aimed at specific changes.
  • Proven methods. Including cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) and cognitive processing therapy (CPT) for PTSD.
  • Respect for who you are. Care that takes your culture, values and history into account.
  • Part of your whole health. With your permission, Dr. Sharp works alongside your doctors and other providers.

What to expect, honestly

Therapy works best as a partnership, and it can bring up hard feelings along the way. Many people find it leads to less distress and better ways to handle stress. Results vary, and there are no guarantees.
