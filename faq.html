FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Can I book BrainTap sessions directly?

Yes. Start with Pathway 2: a short safety screening, a NeuralChek reading and your first BrainTap session. Or start with Pathway 1, a private consultation with Dr. Sharp, if you’d like his input first.

Who runs the sessions?

Certified associates run NeuralChek readings and BrainTap sessions in our Henderson office, in a program Dr. Sharp designed. Counseling is with Dr. Sharp.

When does the office open?

Our Henderson office opens in January. You can request your first visit now.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Therapy is available by telehealth for clients in Nevada, Utah and Idaho. NeuralChek and BrainTap sessions take place in our Henderson office.

Who is BrainTap a good fit for?

Most adults looking for a simple way to relax. A short safety screening before your first session makes sure it’s right for you.

Can I use BrainTap at home?

Yes. You can buy a BrainTap headset and app in our office at a discount, and you don’t need to be a client. Walk-ins are welcome; call ahead to check inventory. You can also shop online through our partner link on the NeuralChek and BrainTap page. Integrated Wellness & Counseling earns a commission on purchases, and buying a device is never required.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We keep what you share private and confidential, within the limits of the law. Everyone who comes to us can count on our discretion.

Fees and insurance

How much does it cost?

We’ll go over pricing for NeuralChek, BrainTap and counseling when we call, before you book anything.

Do you take insurance?

We’re a private-pay practice and don’t bill insurance directly. Many therapy clients submit for out-of-network reimbursement using a detailed receipt we provide. BrainTap and NeuralChek sessions aren’t covered by insurance.

What’s a superbill, and how do I use it?

A superbill is a detailed receipt your insurer needs to process a claim. Ask us for one after you pay, then submit it yourself or through the Reimbursify app. Insurers usually take three to four weeks, and coverage depends on your plan.

How do I pay?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover and American Express through a secure online invoice. PayPal, Zelle and Venmo are available on request. Payment is due at or before each visit.

Still have questions?

We’re happy to talk them through.
