About Dr. Sharp

Meet Dr. Stephen Sharp

A licensed counselor with doctoral and master’s degrees in professional counseling and more than 25 years of experience helping people stay steady under pressure.

At a glance

  • More than 25 years of clinical practice
  • Certified trauma therapist
  • Clinical training from McLean Hospital, a Harvard Medical School affiliate
  • Vice Chair Emeritus, National Board for Certified Counselors
  • Nearly two decades teaching future counselors, including as Academic Dean and Associate Professor
  • Licensed in Nevada, Utah and Idaho, with offices in Henderson, NV, and telehealth in all three states

Experience under pressure

Dr. Sharp has spent his career with people who do hard things under pressure, including first responders, veterans and frontline professionals. He has also trained hundreds of people around the world to deliver mental health support.

How Dr. Sharp works

Every plan is built around one person: you.

  • Focused on your goals. Brief, practical work aimed at real change, not years of open-ended sessions.
  • Trauma-informed. Care that respects what you’ve been through and moves at a pace that feels safe.
  • The right tool for the job. Dr. Sharp designed our NeuralChek and BrainTap program and leads our counseling. Whichever pathway you start on, we’ll point you to what fits.

A note from Dr. Sharp

After more than 25 years in this work, I’ve learned what moves the needle and what doesn’t. Meditation, breath and focused attention have helped people for thousands of years. Today, tools like BrainTap can help you reach those calm, focused states more easily, in as little as 20 minutes a day.

I’m open to new tools, but I’m careful with them. That’s why every BrainTap client is screened first, and why we’ll tell you if counseling would help more.

Stress isn’t an occasional hurdle anymore. For many people, it’s constant. BrainTap gives you a simple way to step out of it and reset.

Dr. Stephen Sharp
